Why Vaultr Exists

Built for the way a collection actually grows.

Vaultr started with my own collection: a few grails I was actively hunting, a wishlist that kept growing, and the same searches I kept checking over and over. I wanted an easier way to keep those chases organized without taking the fun out of collecting.

Weekly Shelf grew out of the other side of collecting too: finding something you didn't know you wanted until you saw it.
