Why KeepSeek Exists

KeepSeek started with my own collection: a growing wishlist, a few grails I was chasing, and the same searches I kept checking again and again. I wanted an easier way to keep track of what I was looking for and surface listings actually worth considering.

It also came from the part of collecting I love most: discovering a card I wasn't even looking for, but instantly knew I wanted. KeepSeek is built from that collector mindset — part practical tool, part discovery.
