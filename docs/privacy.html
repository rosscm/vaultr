Privacy Policy

This policy explains the limited information KeepSeek processes to deliver chase alerts, build Display Case discovery, support collectors, and keep the service reliable.

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Overview

KeepSeek provides collector software for tracking specific card chases, reviewing marketplace matches in a web Inbox, and browsing prepared Display Case recommendations.

Information we process

To operate the service, KeepSeek may process limited information such as:

  • Account and linked sign-in identifiers, including email or Discord identifiers when you use those options
  • Session information used to keep you signed in securely
  • Saved chases, plan state, alert settings, and source preferences
  • Feedback actions such as More Like, Not For Me, tune-outs, and related discovery state
  • Prepared Display Case rows, taste profile memory, and related ranking context
  • Operational logs, job state, and backup metadata used to keep the service healthy

How information is used

  • To provide your Watchlist, Inbox, settings, and Display Case in the web app
  • To deliver optional Discord notifications when an account is connected and opted in
  • To store and apply your Watchlist preferences and discovery feedback
  • To maintain service reliability, freshness, abuse prevention, and diagnostics
  • To administer access, plans, and future billing support

Third-party services

KeepSeek relies on third-party services including authentication and email providers, Discord when connected, eBay, supported shop sources, hosting providers, and future payment providers. Information may be processed by those providers as required to operate the service.

Data retention

Watchlist data, feedback history, prepared shelves, and diagnostic logs may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain reliability, resolve issues, and meet legal obligations.

Applicable law

This policy is intended to be interpreted in accordance with the laws of Canada, unless otherwise required by applicable local privacy or consumer protection law.
