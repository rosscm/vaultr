## Token Efficiency

- Minimize unnecessary exploration and tool calls.
- Inspect only files relevant to the requested task.
- Prefer focused diffs over broad repository audits.
- Make the smallest change that fully addresses the issue.
- Batch related fixes instead of creating multiple passes.
- Run focused tests; avoid redundant validation.
- Keep final reports concise: changes, validation, blockers.
- Do not perform unrelated refactoring or improvements.
- Do not commit, push, deploy, or restart services unless requested.
